GDPR & Data Protection

Last updated: 03 February 2026

We take data protection seriously. This page summarises how we handle personal data and your rights under UK GDPR.

Your rights

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restriction
  • Right to data portability (where applicable)

How to exercise your rights

Email privacy@xentri.co.uk with your request. We may need to verify your identity before processing requests.

Candidate consent

We will not share your CV or profile with a client without your consent.

Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for recruitment activities and legal obligations.
