Privacy Notice

Last updated: 03 February 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how Xentri Ltd (“Xentri”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses personal data when you visit this website, submit an enquiry, or apply for a role.

1. Who we are

Controller: Xentri Ltd (UK). Contact: privacy@xentri.co.uk.

2. What data we collect

  • Contact details (name, email, phone)
  • Recruitment data (CV, work history, skills, availability, salary/rate expectations)
  • Website usage data (basic logs required to operate the site)

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to enquiries
  • To assess suitability for roles and match candidates to opportunities
  • To share candidate profiles with clients only with your consent
  • To improve our services and website

4. Lawful basis

We typically rely on legitimate interests to provide recruitment services, and on consent where required (e.g., sharing your CV with a client or retaining your profile beyond an initial period).

5. Data sharing

We may share data with:

  • Clients (only with consent)
  • Service providers (e.g., form handling, email, hosting)
  • Authorities where legally required

6. Retention

We keep candidate data for a limited period (e.g., up to 12 months) unless you ask us to delete it sooner or agree to longer retention.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to certain processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls and secure transport (HTTPS).

9. Contact

Email: privacy@xentri.co.uk
