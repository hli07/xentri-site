Who we are

Xentri is a specialist recruitment partner focused on engineering and platform roles.

We work with teams building and operating modern cloud platforms — from DevOps and SRE to platform engineering, security, and data infrastructure.

We keep things simple: clear role briefs, realistic timelines, and honest feedback.

What we value

  • Quality over volume: smaller shortlists, better matches.
  • Transparency: no surprises for clients or candidates.
  • Consent-first: we never share CVs without permission.
